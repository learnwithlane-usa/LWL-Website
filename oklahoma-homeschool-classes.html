Creature Card Lab

An English class where kids invent their own creature cards and write everything on them: the name, the stats, the moves, the battle rules and the pitch that wins a trade.

Six sessions, one card set

Each 45-minute session builds one part of the card, and one kind of writing.

Session 1

Invent the creature

Read real cards closely, learn how a card works, then build a name from word parts like aqua, pyro and -saur.

Session 2

Describe it

Precise adjectives and sensory words turn into flavor text that shows instead of tells.

Session 3

Moves and stats

Strong verbs name the moves. Kids balance the numbers and read each other’s cards.

Session 4

Write the rules

Procedural writing: clear battle rules, tested in a real playtest.

Session 5

Make the trade

Persuasive writing: claim, reason and evidence, then a class trade show.

Session 6

Finale

A one-page rulebook to keep, a battle tournament, and the printed card reveal.

The part kids remember

Their creature, on a real card, mailed home

Every student’s finished card is printed on card stock, sealed in a clear display case with a label they design in class, and mailed to your door. It is proof of what they wrote, and something they will want to show everyone.
