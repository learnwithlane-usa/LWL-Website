Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 9, 2026

Learn with Lane ("we," "our," or "us"), founded and operated by David Lane, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website at https://learnwithlane.com, utilize our online tutoring services, purchase our educational printables, or interact with our integrated applications and social channels.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you express an interest in obtaining information about our services, register for tutoring, purchase educational workbooks, or contact us directly. This includes:

  • Personal Contact Details: Name, email address, phone number, and messaging identifiers (e.g., Line, WeChat, WhatsApp) provided when booking trial lessons or submitting inquiries.
  • Student Profile & Educational Goals: Grade level, reading goals, and instructional preferences provided by parents or guardians to customize tutoring lessons.
  • Billing Information: Payment transactions are handled directly through secure third-party payment processors (such as Stripe). We do not collect or store your full credit card numbers.
  • Automatically Collected Usage Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and site interaction metrics collected via Google Analytics (GA4), Meta Pixel, and Pinterest conversion tags.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for legitimate educational and business purposes, including:

  • Delivering, scheduling, and personalizing one-on-one reading remediation and ESL tutoring sessions.
  • Fulfilling orders for digital educational materials, workbooks, and printable curriculum kits.
  • Communicating scheduling reminders, lesson feedback, and customer support responses.
  • Analyzing website traffic and improving our educational resources.
  • Publishing educational pins, scheduling visual learning resources, and syncing rich product pins across Pinterest boards using the Pinterest API.

3. Pinterest API & Third-Party Integrations

Our website and internal management applications connect with third-party service providers and APIs, including the Pinterest API:

  • Pinterest Developer API: Learn with Lane uses the Pinterest API strictly to publish educational printables, manage Pinterest boards, and review pin engagement analytics. We do not sell, rent, or transfer Pinterest user data, nor do we use Pinterest API access for unauthorized profiling or advertising outside of Pinterest's terms.
  • Google Analytics & Tag Manager: We use Google Analytics to monitor aggregated site traffic. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking through Google's browser add-on.
  • Social Media & Advertising Tags: We use conversion tags (such as Pinterest Tag and Meta Pixel) to measure the effectiveness of our educational outreach.

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Learn with Lane takes children’s privacy seriously. We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13. All tutoring services, consultations, account registrations, and digital workbook purchases must be booked and authorized by a parent, legal guardian, or adult educator. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13 without parental consent, please contact us immediately so we can promptly delete such information.

5. Data Retention & Security

We maintain reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill the tutoring and instructional services requested, comply with legal obligations, and resolve any disputes.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. You may also unsubscribe from our email communications at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in our emails or by contacting us directly.
