Reading Remediation

Targeted, intensive intervention for students who need to close reading gaps. Perfect for overcoming hurdles in phonemic awareness, reading fluency, and reading comprehension.

  • Rate: $15 per session.

English as a Second Language (ESL)

Dynamic and conversational English coaching for global learners. Designed to help students master pronunciation, expand vocabulary, and build practical communication skills.

  • Rate: $10 per session.
