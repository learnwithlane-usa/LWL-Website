About me

Twenty years of teaching, one kitchen table

I have spent more than 20 years in the classroom. I hold a Master’s degree in Educational Leadership and a TESOL certification, and I live in Mead, Oklahoma, near Lake Texoma.

I also homeschool my own teenager, so I know what it takes to keep a kid interested day after day. That is why my lessons start with something kids already care about, and why every worksheet teaches one skill at a time. My goal is simple: a student who feels safe, encouraged and ready to try.
